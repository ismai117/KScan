BarcodeFormat

enum BarcodeFormat : Enum<BarcodeFormat>

The formats ScannerView and scanImage can be asked to scan for.

FORMAT_ALL_FORMATS selects every format the platform supports. TYPE_UNKNOWN is reported for a format the platform decoder does not map.
